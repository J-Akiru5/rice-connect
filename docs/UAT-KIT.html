RiceConnect test kit: team round and interviews

Team Syntaxure Labs · ISUFST. Code freeze Oct 5; interviews Oct 6–7; contest Oct 9.

TL;DR
  • Run a 30–40 minute team test round on Oct 4: one tester per role, scripted tasks, a scribe filling the sheet.
  • On Oct 6–7, show interviewees the same flows (5 minutes each), read the consent note first, and record only anonymous feedback.
  • Everything is simulated: no real farmers, buyers, prices or locations. Say so out loud.
  • Triage: anything that blocks a task or shows a wrong number is S1 and gets fixed before Oct 5 freeze.

1. Before you start

Device mixOne laptop (1440 or wider), one Android phone (Chrome), one tablet or a laptop window around 1024px.
Fresh stateOpen the site in a private window per tester, so saved orders, language and theme start clean.
RolesFacilitator (reads tasks, does not help), tester, scribe (fills the sheet). Rotate.
TimeboxOne minute of silence before helping. Note where the tester hesitated, not only where they failed.

Everyone, once

  • Switch to Dark and back. Switch language to TL and HIL on two screens: nothing overflows or is cut off.
  • Use only the keyboard (Tab, Enter, arrows) on /farm: every control shows a visible focus ring.
  • Reload in the middle of a filtered list and press Back: the list state comes back.

3. Acceptance criteria (what "ready for interviews" means)

AreaCriterionMet
TasksAll S1 tasks above pass for at least 2 of 3 testers without help.
NumbersNo on-screen number differs from docs/NUMBERS.md; assumed values are labeled "assumed" on screen.
LayoutNo sideways page scroll at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, 1920 (see docs/RESPONSIVE-CHECKLIST.md).
Honesty"Prototype · Simulated data" chip visible on every screen; nobody leaves thinking the data is real.
LanguageTL/HIL shown as drafts ("Draft: needs native review"); a native speaker has read the SMS before farmers see them.
PrivacyNo real names, phone numbers or exact locations entered anywhere during testing.

4. Consent note (read before showing the prototype in an interview)

"Thank you for your time. We are students from ISUFST testing an early prototype called RiceConnect. Everything you will see is simulated: the farms, names, prices and places are made up. We would like to show you a few screens and ask what you think.

We will not record your name, phone number or the name of your business. We will only write down your comments and your role (for example, restaurant owner or rice retailer). You can stop at any time, and you can skip any question.

Is it okay to continue?"

Tagalog and Hiligaynon versions: have a native speaker translate this note before the interviews; the app's own TL/HIL text is still a draft.

Severity

S1 · BlockerTask cannot be finished, a number is wrong, or something looks real that is not. Fix before Oct 5.
S2 · MajorTask finished only with help, or text overflows / is unreadable. Fix if time allows.
S3 · MinorWording, spacing, wishes. Log for after the contest.

Interview questions (after the walkthrough)

  1. Who do you buy rice or palay from today, and what is the hardest part?
  2. Would knowing supply by week and barangay change how you order? What else would you need to see?
  3. Would you trust a lot more if you could trace it to the farm? What would make you trust it?
  4. What would stop you from using this?
