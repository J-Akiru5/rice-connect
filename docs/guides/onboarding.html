Onboarding · one page per role

RiceConnect onboarding guides

Team Syntaxure Labs · ISUFST. Print one page per person, in their language where possible. Tagalog and Hiligaynon lines below are drafts pending native review (S-13); the team reads them aloud until reviewed.

Support: call or text __________ (staffed during pilot hours, ____–____). If the app misbehaves, stop and call; the paper fallback keeps the day running.

Coordinator

  1. Sign in at the coordinator page with your email and password. Change the password if you were given a temporary one.
  2. Start the day on Home. It lists this week's harvests, any haul waiting for a driver, advances due and new buyer orders.
  3. Register a farm when a farmer joins: Add Farm, name, barangay, area, variety, harvest week. One farm, one farmer.
  4. Weigh and record a lot when palay arrives; the lot number follows the farm.
  5. Book the dryer from Dry: check free capacity before assigning a slot; never overbook a day.
  6. Assign the haul from Logistics: the app suggests the nearest driver whose vehicle fits; you can override, and the override stays in the history.
  7. Mark paid from Orders once the advance or balance is actually sent. This is irreversible in the app: confirm the amount with the slip first.
  8. Answer replies from SMS: "1 OK" confirms the slot, "2 Move" asks to move it.

If the app is down: use the paper slot sheet, haul sheet and slip from the fallback pack, and enter them when service returns.

Farmer

  1. The account is made for you by the coordinator; you get SMS from RiceConnect, not an app to install.
  2. When you get a message about your lot and dryer slot, reply 1 to agree, or reply 2 to ask to move it.
  3. You also get a message when the advance is sent, and another when the balance is released.
  4. Keep your slip (paper or the printed copy from the coordinator). It shows the net amount, the 80% advance and the 20% balance.
  5. If a message looks wrong, call the support number before the harvest.

Draft lines: "Sagot 1 kung sang-ayon, 2 kung gusto ilipat." / "Sabat 1 kon nagauyon, 2 kon gusto ibalhin."

Driver

  1. Sign in with your mobile number and password.
  2. Your job card shows the haul, the pickup date, the sacks and what you earn. Accept when you can take it, or Decline and the coordinator assigns another driver.
  3. At the farm, press Mark Picked Up; at the dryer or buyer, press Mark Delivered.
  4. No signal? The app keeps the step as "Not sent yet" and sends it when the signal returns. Do not press twice.
  5. If the load is not ready, call the coordinator instead of waiting.

Draft lines: "Kunin" / "Kuhaa" for accept; "Ihatid" / "Ihatod" for deliver.

Buyer

  1. Sign in with your email and password, and choose what you buy as: Miller, Retailer, Market Seller or Restaurant.
  2. The Supply page shows what the cluster expects per week, and the map shows supply per barangay.
  3. Order rice: choose sacks and week, review the summary, place the order. You have a short Undo window after placing.
  4. Track the order from Orders: it traces back to the lot and the farm code (no farmer identity).
  5. Millers post a standing commitment (tonnes, price, weeks) instead of a rice order.
